# Changelog

All notable changes to **Farm Freeze** (`FS25_FreezeInactiveFarm`) are documented here.

## 1.0.0.0

- Initial release.
- Freezes, while no manager of a farm is online: bank balance, loans (including Bank & Credit), vehicle age and operating hours, animal time (aging, reproduction, health), feed, water and straw in husbandries, husbandry production (eggs, milk, wool), and manure and slurry.
- In-game settings menu with a switch for every feature (server admin only, synced to all players), in English and Polish.
